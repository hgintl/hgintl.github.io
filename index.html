About the Firm

H&G International Group LLC is a privately held investment firm. We deploy private equity and hedge fund capital across sports, hospitality, and gaming — from landmark resorts and casinos to the stadiums, arenas, and brands around them.

Our capital is patient, our structures are flexible, and our work is private. We partner with owners, operators, and institutions on transactions where focus and discretion matter.

  • Private Equity — control and growth investments in hospitality and gaming assets
  • Hedge Fund — opportunistic capital across public and private markets in the sector
  • Sports & Live Entertainment — stadiums, arenas, venues, and live-event platforms
  • Development — site acquisition and ground-up development, including stadium-anchored commercial mixed-use districts
  • Advisory — gaming licensing and strategic counsel for partners and portfolio companies

02
